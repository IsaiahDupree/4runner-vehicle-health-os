RGBW output terminals

JT4-1 R− · JT4-2 G− · JT5-1 B− · JT5-2 common + · JT6-1 W−. JT6-2 stays empty. Match pad labels, not wire colors.

First power and arming

Set a private Wi-Fi password and relay key in WLED. Verify the board and exact strip with a current-limited bench supply before enabling arm-output in WLED → Usermods → RevG. Calibration does not approve the strip or increase the current limit.

Values below are ESP32 ADC millivolts. They are not voltages at the Toyota wire.

Measure both states. At least 50 mV separation; either polarity supported. Changes must remain steady for 200 ms.

Measure a span of at least 100 mV and characterize the truck's DIM waveform before enabling. Default: both controls disabled.
